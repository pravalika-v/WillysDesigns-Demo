Demo privacy notice

This website is a demonstration. Please use sample information when testing the enquiry form.

The form stores your name, email address, phone number, project type, message, submission time and a reference in a private database on the hosting service. The site operator can review these records to test the enquiry workflow. Email notifications are not enabled.

The enquiry database is not publicly downloadable. Rate limiting uses connection information temporarily in memory. The hosting provider may separately keep access logs.

Calling or opening WhatsApp uses an external service. Do not include sensitive information in test enquiries. Ask the demo operator to remove any real details submitted accidentally.

This notice describes the demo only. Confirm the business contact information, retention policy and production privacy notice before collecting real customer enquiries.
