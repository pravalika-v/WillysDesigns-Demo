Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most. If yours isn't here, call us or send a message and we will help.
What is 3D desing and how it work?
We turn your 2D plan into a realistic 3D view of the finished room, with furniture, colours and materials. You can review it and ask for changes before anything is built.
How interior design is cost?
It depends on the room size, materials and scope. After the first consultation we share a written estimate before any work begins.
How much time I will spend on planning?
Planning is usually quicker than people expect. We handle measuring, layouts and sourcing, and only need your decisions at a few review points.
Can I create custom design?
Yes. We design pieces and layouts to your room's measurements and style, and manage the build and installation.
